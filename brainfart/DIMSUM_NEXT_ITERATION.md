# Dim Sum: Next Iteration

Feedback from the call with Dustin. Dim Sum is the new name for the Brainfart prototype.

**Goal for this round:** a tighter build that's good enough to put in front of 20 to 25 beta testers.

---

## 1. Rename to Dim Sum

- [ ] Replace "Brainfart" with **Dim Sum** everywhere (wordmark, onboarding, title, copy).
- [ ] Check that no other app is already called Dim Sum, especially in learning or video. Dustin asked for this before launch.
- Rejected names: BrainPop (already an established learning brand), Lightbulb (too generic), Aha, Eureka, Daily Spark, One Idea, Midnight Class.

## 2. Video lengths

- [ ] Add a **1 minute** option to serve people who prefer short content.
- [ ] Choose the final set. Options discussed:
  - **1 / 5 / 10 / 15**
  - **1 / 5 / 15** (10 felt a little arbitrary)
  - Not decided yet; either works for beta.
- [ ] Ask for a preferred length during onboarding.
- [ ] Make switching length **instant and always visible**, including mid-session ("actually, I want it a little longer").

## 3. Daily program vs. endless feed

- [ ] Decide between a program that ends and a feed you can keep watching. On the call the lean was to **let people keep going as long as they want**.
- [ ] Consider a switch between the two modes.
- Note: this changes the "the program ends" pitch in the current prototype and the competitor one-pager. Update both once it's decided.

## 4. Categories: Arts, Sciences, Civics

The current list is too heavy on science and missing the arts.

- [ ] Restructure into **three top-level columns**:
  - **Arts:** music, painting, sculpture, poetry, and more (make it thorough)
  - **Sciences:** physics, math, biology, chemistry, ecology, space, acoustics, etc.
  - **Civics:** politics, government, society. Keep it as its own column so it doesn't get lost inside Sciences.
- [ ] Make the lists under each column thorough and well organized.
- [ ] Tighten the onboarding picker. "Still a lot of bubbles" is how it read.

## 5. Feed + Chat

- [ ] **Feed** is the default screen.
- [ ] Add a **Chat** tab where you can ask anything and talk to the app, including by voice.
- [ ] Use **Gemini** to power it (for showing it to Google contacts). Model choice can come later.
- [ ] Give it **memory** of the user plus a "soul" profile: their interests, beliefs, and how they like to learn.
- Reference: Meta's Muse has chat, feed, ideas, goals, and library. Download it and match its ease of use.

## 6. Content strategy

- [ ] **Human-first.** Recruit creators who already make this kind of content and have them add Dim Sum as one more channel in their existing cross-posting setup.
- [ ] **Label AI-generated content clearly**, possibly as a separate feed. People are likely to react badly to anything that isn't made by a person.
- [ ] Running an all-AI version as an experiment is fine, but keep it next to the human content, not in place of it.
- [ ] Don't plan on pulling content from Instagram. There's no API that allows it. TikTok's terms still need checking.
- [ ] Don't lock the look to a single style, such as all animation.
- For now the "videos" are code-generated visuals. Photorealistic AI video comes later because of compute cost (Dustin has a Google plan that could cover it).

## 7. Positioning

- **The anti-doomscroll:** a positive social platform for learning.
- **"What if Pioneer Works were a little social app?"** The full range of learning, fun and cool, the opposite of brain rot.
- Competitors to watch: **Nebula** (content), **Kinnu** (mechanics), and **TikTok's STEM feed**.
  - [ ] Research how much TikTok STEM is actually used in the US. If few people use it, that supports building a separate app instead of a TikTok feed.
  - Correction: Nebula is owned by its creators, not by Google.
- Possible partner or funder later: Anthropic, because of its values. Not a goal right now.

## 8. Launch plan

- [ ] Do one or two more passes on the UX, then share with beta testers.
- [ ] Line up 20 to 25 testers (Dustin's target: 25 users by tomorrow, starting with friends and his girlfriend).
- [ ] Add an easy way for testers to give feedback.
- Timing: Dustin wants to go to market today; Justin suggested a few more rounds first. Agreed for now: a better version by end of day, then decide.
